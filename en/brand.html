02Color

Colour

The five neutrals lean slightly warm rather than pure grey. Gold stays under 1% of any screen.

  • ink#111113Primary text, symbol cells, primary button fill
  • graphite#1C1C1FDark secondary text (figures beside headings)
  • slate#2E2E33Body text, strong rules
  • mist#A3A3AD1px rules (50% alpha), placeholder text
  • paper#F4F3F0Page background; cards use rules, not fills
  • gold#C9A961Accent on dark
  • gold-deep#A8863FAccent on light
