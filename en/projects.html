R&D

Research projects · systems

  • GENERATIVE AIOngoing

    Improving Generative AI Models for Cultural Content Production

    Virtual Convergence Technology Research Institute R&D · 2025 –

    Raising the quality and controllability of generative models used in cultural content production pipelines: conditional control and editing methods for image and video generation.

  • GENERATIVE AIOngoing

    Generative AI for Content Production Workflows

    Industry–academia joint R&D · Dongseo University with Themantastory · 2026 –

    A three-year R&D programme, based on an industry needs survey, applying generative AI to content production. Aims at on-site use of image and video generation models and follow-on government funding.

  • LLM · AGENTSOngoing

    Local LLM Multi-Agent System

    Lab project · 2026 –

    Language-model agents that do not depend on the cloud: four agents — orchestrator, research, development, and assistant — run on local models and connect through a messenger. Open-weight model selection and serving optimisation are the core.

  • GENERATIVE AIOngoing

    ComfyUI Content Generation Pipeline

    Lab project · 2026 –

    Text-to-image, video, and speech generation plus lip-sync video production in one workflow. Runs headless on a remote GPU server, with long-audio segmentation and Korean speech synthesis.

  • INFRASTRUCTURECompleted

    Lab AI Server

    Lab project · 2026

    An open-weight LLM and generative-model serving environment on a GPU server. Coding agents connect to local models through vLLM and Ollama; lab members access it remotely.
